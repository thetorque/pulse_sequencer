Keep separateThe sideband is a dedicated SPI1, not the config SPI0 — sharing would put runtime traffic on PROGRAMN/CS and risk an accidental reconfig. AD9912 SPI is not on the Pico: the Pico sends setpoints over the sideband and the ECP5 executes them.
